What managers can do

01 / Staff and stock for 8 to 10 AM first

Put people, prepared ingredients and replenishment where the morning demand is concentrated.

02 / Plan coffee and tea inventory first

These two categories account for two thirds of revenue across the full six-month period.

03 / Start Lower Manhattan early

Give Lower Manhattan a 6 to 7 AM shift. Hell's Kitchen peaks hard at 8 to 10, then roughly halves at 11.

04 / Validate the traffic growth

Investigate the Feb to Jun traffic growth before assuming it continues. Check demand drivers before extending staffing commitments.
